# Original public character and icons from PixelLab

The full-body reference was generated from text with PixelLab on 15 October
2026. No third-party character or personal artwork was supplied as a reference.
On 16 October, that approved image was the input for 16 animations and a bust
portrait. The user compass was generated with PixelLab on 15 October.

`provenance.json` records prompts, seeds, provider job/asset identifiers and
source hashes. The PNG atlases here preserve the pixels of every downloaded
frame. `tools/build_character.py` performs offline technical packaging: a
10-pixel downward translation, exact rest bookends, state badges, atlas assembly,
portrait padding, nearest-neighbor icon sizes and ICO encoding. It does not
draw or generate the character. The runtime assets in
`assets/pixellab/production/pet/`, `assets/public/pet-reference.png`, the face
icons in `src-tauri/icons/` and the avatars in `assets/branding/` derive from
these PixelLab outputs. The unchanged reference remains in
`assets/character-preview/` with its original generation provenance.

These AI-generated images and their derived media are excluded from the code's
MIT grant. PixelLab's [terms](https://www.pixellab.ai/termsofservice), checked
on 16 October 2026, permit commercial and non-commercial use and distribution
of outputs and restrict using generated images to train models without
permission. They refer to
[CreativeML Open RAIL-M](https://github.com/CompVis/stable-diffusion/blob/main/LICENSE).
Retain the notices and provenance when redistributing these assets. No model
weights or provider software are included.

The separate 42 geometric UI glyphs, five state badges and eight panel textures
are original project geometry under MIT; they were not generated by PixelLab.
